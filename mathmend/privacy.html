MathMend privacy policy

Last updated: [CONFIRM date]

MathMend is developed and published by Sky Fire Studios Limited, Nairobi, Kenya (“we”). This policy explains what information MathMend handles and why.

Information we handle

  • Account details: the sign-in details you provide through our sign-in provider (Google Firebase Authentication). [CONFIRM sign-in methods offered]
  • Learning content: questions you type, photos or documents you upload, your working, solutions, mistakes, practice and progress.
  • Technical information: limited diagnostic and security information needed to run and protect the service. [CONFIRM analytics or crash-reporting tools used]

How we use it

To check and explain maths, remember mistakes and build practice, keep accounts secure, provide support, and improve reliability.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show personalised advertising in learning screens.
  • We do not use student photos or work to train AI models by default. [CONFIRM provider settings for any AI or OCR service used]

Service providers

Children

MathMend is designed for school learners. Where the law requires, we need permission from a parent, guardian or school before processing a child’s information. [CONFIRM minimum age and consent process; Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and other launch countries]

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by contacting us.
