How it works

From “I got it wrong” to “I get it” in four steps

  1. Type or snap your question

    Type it in, or take a photo of the question and your working.

  2. MathMend checks every step

    Supported maths is checked by a maths engine, so steps are verified rather than guessed.

  3. See the first real mistake

    It points to the line where the method went wrong and explains why, without burying you in later errors.

  4. Practise and remember

    Your mistakes are remembered and turned into practice, so you stop repeating them.

How MathMend responds to your working

Two examples of what MathMend does with a supported problem.

When there is a mistake
QuestionSolve 3(x − 2) = 2x + 5
3x − 2 = 2x + 5First mistake
x − 2 = 5follows line 1
x = 7follows line 1
Line 1: 3(x − 2) means 3x − 6, because the 3 multiplies both terms in the bracket. Continue from 3x − 6 = 2x + 5 to get x = 11.
When your method is different but valid
QuestionSolve 3(x − 2) = 2x + 5
3x − 6 = 2x + 5valid
x = 11valid
All steps valid. You combined steps, and that is fine. Answer checked by substitution: 3(11 − 2) = 27 and 2(11) + 5 = 27.
