How it works

  1. Type or snap your question

    Type it in, or take a photo of the question and your working.

  2. MathMend checks every step

    Supported maths is checked by a maths engine, so steps are verified rather than guessed.

  3. See the first real mistake

    It points to the line where the method went wrong and explains why, without burying you in later errors.

  4. Practise and remember

    Your mistakes are remembered and turned into practice, so you stop repeating them.

Why students and teachers choose MathMend

Checks your method, not just the answer

Valid alternative methods are accepted. The aim is to understand where your thinking went wrong.

Explains it twice if you need it

Every solution ends with “Did you understand?”. Tap No and the same solution is explained again, more simply.

Learning, not copying

Built to help you learn, with practice from your own mistakes and progress you can see.

Honest when unsure

If MathMend can’t read a question clearly or a topic isn’t supported yet, it says so instead of guessing.
