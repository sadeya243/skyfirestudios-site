Trust

How MathMend keeps maths honest and learners safe

What “verified” means

For supported topics, MathMend checks answers and steps with a maths engine, and confirms solutions independently, for example by substituting the answer back into the question. A language model is not trusted as the only source of mathematical truth.

When MathMend is not sure

If a photo is unclear, the notation is ambiguous or a topic is not supported yet, MathMend asks you to confirm or tells you it cannot help yet, instead of guessing.

Found a mistake?

Tell us which question and which step. Every confirmed error becomes a permanent test so it does not come back. Report an error.
