Roadmap

Beyond maths: the sciences are next

MathMend starts with mathematics. Over time we plan to bring the same approach to the sciences: checked calculations, clear explanations and practice built from each learner’s mistakes.

Planned

Physics

Motion, forces, energy, electricity and waves, with units and formulas checked step by step.

Planned

Chemistry

Moles, concentrations, gas laws and balancing equations, plus explanations of key concepts.

Planned

Biology

Genetics and data calculations, and feedback on written answers against the key points examiners look for.

Later

More subjects

Further subjects as MathMend grows, always starting with what we can check and explain reliably.

Planned subjects are not available yet and have no release date. We will announce each one here when it is ready.
