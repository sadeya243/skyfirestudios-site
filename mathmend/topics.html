Curriculum guides

MathMend covers mathematics taught in schools around the world. Guides that map our topics to major curricula are being prepared, including Cambridge IGCSE and A Level, the International Baccalaureate (IB), UK GCSE, US Common Core and SAT maths, India’s CBSE, and other national curricula around the world.

Schools in our pilot get early access and can ask for their curriculum to be mapped first.

Coverage changes as we add and test topics. This page is updated when a topic moves to fully tested.
